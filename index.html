About Corex Team

Technology Should Serve People

Founded in 2025, Corex Team is an independent software development initiative built around a simple idea: technology should respect the people who use it.

We want to develop software that is private, efficient, accessible, and useful in everyday life. We don't believe applications need to collect unnecessary information, force unwanted features on users, or make people dependent on a single provider.

We value human creativity and responsible engineering. AI tools can help us work more efficiently as a small team, but technology should support human work rather than replace human judgement, originality, and responsibility.

We also want to contribute to a world where people and organisations have more freedom to choose their technology. From Europe to the rest of the world, we believe independent software and open standards can help reduce unnecessary dependency on dominant technology providers.

What We Believe In

Our Goals

We have a simple vision: protect people's privacy, develop independent software, preserve human creativity, and help build a more digitally independent future.

01 / Privacy

Privacy First

Your data belongs to you. We don't believe in collecting personal information just because we can, and we don't believe in selling people's data. Our goal is to build software that respects your privacy, collects only what is genuinely necessary, and keeps you in control. Privacy shouldn't be a premium feature.

02 / Software

Independent Software

We want to build independent, efficient software with features that solve real problems. We value transparency, open standards, and giving people the freedom to choose their own tools. Software should work for its users — without unnecessary complexity, unwanted features, or forced dependence on a single company.

03 / Creativity

Human Creativity Matters

We recognise that AI can be useful, especially for small development teams with limited resources. But we believe technology should support people, not replace their creativity and judgement. We value original ideas, genuine effort, thoughtful engineering, and people who take responsibility for the work they create.

04 / Independence

Digital Independence

We want Europe and the rest of the world to have more freedom in choosing the technologies they depend on. By supporting independent software, open standards, and open-source projects, we aim to help reduce vendor lock-in and unnecessary dependence on a small number of dominant technology providers. We are not against people from any country; we stand for freedom of choice.

These principles guide what we want Corex Team to become.
