Features

Everything you need to write a document

Docu keeps the tools you already know within reach, so you can focus on the writing.

Writing and formatting

A familiar place to write.

Docu uses a ribbon-style interface with Home, Insert, Layout and View tabs. Format text, align paragraphs, and build headings and lists in a document editing area that looks like the page you will print.

  • Fonts, sizes, text color and highlights
  • Bold, italic, underline and more
  • Left, center, right and justified alignment
  • Headings, bulleted lists, numbered lists and checklists

Find and replace

Find it. Change it.

Press Ctrl + F to search the whole document. Matches are highlighted, you can step through them, and you can replace one match or all of them at once.

Options for matching case and whole words help you narrow the search.

Document tools

Small tools that help while you write.

Document outline

Your headings appear in a side panel so you can jump to any part of the document.

Word and character counts

The status bar keeps a running count, and shows how many words are selected.

Estimated reading time

See roughly how many minutes your document takes to read.

Zoom

Zoom in and out, or fit the page to the width of the window.

Focus mode

Hide everything except the page when you want to concentrate.

Light, dark and system themes

Pick a theme in the View tab, or let Docu match your system.

File compatibility and export

Work with the files you already have.

Docu opens and exports DOCX files, and also works with HTML, Markdown and plain text files. For a PDF, use the print dialog and choose Save as PDF.

Open
  • DOCX
  • HTML
  • Markdown
  • TXT
Export
  • DOCX
  • HTML
  • Markdown
  • TXT
  • PDF (via print)

Document recovery

You suddenly closed the document without saving? no need to cry. Corex team took care of it. Docu saves the Document inside itself as temporary file inside executable.

Privacy and local storage

Your documents stay on your device.

Docu keeps your documents on your own device. Export a copy whenever you want one to share or to keep as a backup.

Local by default

Writing, formatting and exporting all happen in the Docu window on your device.
